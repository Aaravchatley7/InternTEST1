# Confidence Calibration Analysis

## Objective

Determine whether confidence scores correlate with validation quality.

---

## Inputs

Validation Results

↓

Confidence Layer

↓

Confidence Score

---

## Confidence Levels

### HIGH

Score >= 0.85

Expected:

Strong validation agreement.

---

### MEDIUM

0.65 <= Score < 0.85

Expected:

Partial agreement.

---

### LOW

Score < 0.65

Expected:

Weak validation agreement.

---

## Explainability

Confidence scores include:

- Reasoning
- Weight Contributions
- Validation Signals
