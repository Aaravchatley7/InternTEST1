# Capability Scope Definition

BHIV Multi-Input Intelligence Platform

---

* Aadhaar Verification
* PAN Verification
* Field Matching
* Confidence Scoring

---

## Document Intelligence

* OCR Extraction
* Field Recovery
* Evidence Generation

---

## Knowledge Intelligence

* PDF Processing
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation
* Context Retrieval
* Question Answering

---

## Observability

* Metrics Collection
* Traceability
* Logging
* Monitoring

---

## Provenance

* Trace IDs
* Request IDs
* Contract Versioning
* Evaluation Versioning

---

# Out of Scope

## Governance

* Regulatory Interpretation
* Policy Decisions
* Approval Workflows

---

## Identity Ownership

* Legal Identity Verification
* Official Certification
* Government Authentication

---

## Execution Authority

* User Approval
* Workflow Authorization
* Administrative Decisions

---

# Intended Usage

The capability is intended to provide:

* Extraction
* Validation
* Confidence
* Evidence

for downstream systems.

---

# Non-Intended Usage

The capability should not be used as:

* Governance Authority
* Compliance Authority
* Legal Authority
* Approval Engine

---

# Conclusion

The platform is an intelligence and validation capability rather than a governance or decision-making authority.
